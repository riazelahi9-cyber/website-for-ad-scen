Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Please read this page carefully. It explains the general terms and information practices for using the RIAZ IDEAS website.

1. About this policy

This policy describes the general information practices for the RIAZ IDEAS website. It applies to information collected through website visits and enquiries submitted using the contact details or embedded inquiry form on this site.

2. Information you provide

If you contact us by email, WhatsApp or the embedded Tally inquiry form, you may choose to provide details such as your name, contact information, business or project details and message. We use these details to review and respond to your enquiry and to communicate about the request.

3. Website analytics and technical information

The website may use analytics and hosting services that process technical information such as pages visited, browser and device details, approximate location derived from network information and general usage events. These details help us understand website performance and maintain the service. The specific data collected depends on the tools enabled at the time of your visit.

4. Cookies and advertising

This website may use Google AdSense or other advertising services. If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalise advertisements, subject to applicable law, consent requirements and the settings available to visitors. You can review Google’s privacy information and advertising controls through Google’s official resources.

5. Third-party service providers

Some website features may be provided by third parties, including form, analytics, hosting and advertising providers. When you use an embedded form or follow an external link, the relevant provider may process information under its own privacy policy and terms. We encourage you to review those policies.

6. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal contact information. Information may be processed by service providers where reasonably needed to operate the website, manage enquiries, maintain security or meet legal obligations. We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary for its purpose or applicable requirements.

7. Your choices and requests

You may choose not to submit personal information, although this may limit our ability to respond to an enquiry. You can manage cookies through your browser and any consent controls made available on the website. To ask about information you have submitted, email riazideas@gmail.com.

8. Children’s privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate permission. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Policy changes

We may revise this policy when website features, service providers or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
